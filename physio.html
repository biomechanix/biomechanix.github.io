Applications
MoveMentor Physio · Rehabilitation

Examine, prescribe and follow up, all with one camera.

MoveMentor Physio is a clinic app for physiotherapists and their patients. Measure how a patient moves, prescribe the right exercises at the right dose, and send them home with a coach that watches every rep.

Get it on Google Play For clinicians For patients
The care loop

One loop from the clinic to the living room and back.

  1. ExamineRun guided movement tests. The camera measures peak range of motion, left–right symmetry and hold time.
  2. PrescribeChoose exercises from the library and set reps, holds and range-of-motion targets for this patient.
  3. DeliverSend the prescription as a QR code or file, or online to a linked patient account.
  4. PerformThe patient exercises at home with real-time form feedback and voice cues at the prescribed dose.
  5. ReviewCompleted sets, reps and peak range come back to you for each prescription.
For clinicians

Objective measures, without a goniometer in your hand.

01

Camera-measured exams

Guided movement tests record achieved range, symmetry between sides and hold duration. Each result is saved as a finding on the patient's record.

02

Dose-adjusted prescriptions

Start from a validated exercise and adjust only what should change: reps, hold time, target range and difficulty. The movement model underneath stays intact.

03

Contraindication checks

Prescriptions are checked against the patient's condition. Absolute contraindications block the exercise. Relative ones and precautions show warnings you can review and dismiss.

04

Author your own exercises

Build exercises and flows in a visual editor: pick the moving joints, set the range, add rest between rounds, then rehearse on camera before you prescribe.

05

Your library, your patients

Exercises you author stay private to you and your patients. Submit an exercise for peer review if you want it added to the shared library.

06

Works offline

Examine, prescribe and share by QR code or file without a connection. Sign in when you want online delivery and adherence reports.

For patients

Your home programme, with a coach watching.

Scan the QR code from your physiotherapist or open the file they sent, and your exercises are ready. Each one runs at the dose you were prescribed, with the same live form feedback and spoken cues as MoveMentor Fitness.

  • Exact reps, holds and targets from your prescription
  • Real-time cues when your form drifts
  • A record of every session against what was prescribed
  • Your results can go back to your clinician, so they can adjust your plan between visits
Privacy

Live analysis stays on the device.

Pose analysis runs on the phone, and camera frames are never uploaded. Patient records created in the clinic are stored on the clinician's device. Video leaves the device only if a clinician deliberately records a clip and creates a share link.

Clinical use

A tool for clinicians, not a replacement.

MoveMentor Physio supports a qualified physiotherapist's assessment and treatment. Exercise selection, dosage and interpretation of measurements remain the clinician's professional judgement.
