How it works

From camera frame to coaching cue, in time for your next rep.

All three apps are built on the same on-device movement engine. It turns a live video feed into joint angles, then compares them with a model of how each movement should look.

  1. SeePose estimation finds 33 body landmarks in every frame, 30 times a second, on your phone.
  2. MeasureLandmarks are converted into joint angles, range of motion, left–right symmetry and hold times.
  3. UnderstandEach exercise or test is modeled as the joint that drives it, the joints that should stay still, and the compensations to watch for.
  4. RespondReps are counted and form is scored. A spoken cue arrives in time to fix the next rep.
Privacy by design

Your camera feed stays on your phone.

Live pose analysis runs on the device, and camera frames are discarded as soon as they're analyzed. Video is saved or shared only when you choose to record or share a clip. We don't do facial recognition, and we don't sell data or use it for advertising. When you sign in, only structured results sync to your account, such as reps, scores and assessment answers.

No extra hardware

Just a phone and a little floor space.

No wearables, sensors or special camera. Stand the phone at chest height, 2–2.5 m away, with your whole body in view, then start moving. The app waits until it can see the joints each movement needs, and pauses if you step out of frame.
