MoveMentor Support

Support for the MoveMentor family of movement-coaching apps by Biomechanix Inc., including MoveMentor Fitness — AI form feedback, rep counting, and voice cues powered by on-device vision.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app upload my camera video?
No. Pose analysis runs entirely on your device. Camera frames are never transmitted or stored — only your workout results (reps, scores, durations) sync to your account.

Do I need any wearables or extra hardware?
No — just your phone's camera.

How do I delete my account and data?
Open a support request (below) from the email address on your account and we will delete your account and all associated workout data. See our privacy policy for details on what we store.

Contact us

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: open a support request — we respond there directly.
