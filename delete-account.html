Delete Your Account

Applies to the MoveMentor apps by Biomechanix Inc., including MoveMentor Fitness. You can delete your account and its data at any time, with or without the app installed.

From inside the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and its data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app (web request)

Open a deletion request telling us the email address on your account. We verify the request against that email and complete the deletion within 30 days; you will receive a confirmation reply on the request.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email and name).
  • All workout records linked to it — sessions, reps, form scores, history.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Nothing is retained afterward: camera video never leaves your device in the first place (see our Privacy Policy), and we keep no analytics or advertising profiles.

Not ready to delete?

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account — use one of the options above. If you only want to stop syncing, sign out from Settings instead.
