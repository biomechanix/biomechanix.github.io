Your data

Delete your account

This applies to all Biomechanix apps: MoveMentor Fitness, MoveMentor Physio and Sameeksha. You can delete your account and its data at any time, whether or not the app is installed.

From inside the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and its data are deleted immediately and you're signed out.

In Sameeksha, you can also delete a single assessment from your history, or use Delete all my data in Settings to remove every assessment from your device and from your account backup.

Without the app (web request)

Email {{ site.contact_email }} from the email address on your account, and tell us which app you used. We verify the request against that address, complete the deletion within 30 days, and confirm when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email and name).
  • MoveMentor Fitness: all workout records linked to it, including sessions, reps, form scores and history.
  • MoveMentor Physio: prescriptions and adherence records stored in your account, exercises you authored, and any clip share links you created.
  • Sameeksha: every assessment backed up to your account.

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. Live camera video never leaves your device in the first place (see our Privacy Policy), and we keep no analytics or advertising profiles.

Data on your device

Deleting your account removes data from our servers. Records stored only on your phone, such as a clinician's patient records in MoveMentor Physio or clips you saved, are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall it. In Sameeksha, you can export your data first from Settings → Privacy & data if you want to keep a copy.

Not ready to delete?

Uninstalling the app doesn't delete your account, so use one of the options above. If you only want to stop syncing, sign out in Settings instead.
