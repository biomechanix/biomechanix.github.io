About Biomechanix

Expert movement coaching, for everyone.

Biomechanix Inc. builds MoveMentor, a movement-intelligence platform that turns a phone camera into a coach. It brings a good physiotherapist's or trainer's eye to wherever people train, recover and move.

About us

Why we exist

Good coaching changes lives, but most people can't get it. Sessions are expensive, experts are busy, and nobody is watching when you practise alone at home or in the gym.

We started Biomechanix to close that gap. Our apps analyze movement on the phone in real time, compare it with how the movement should be done, and tell you what to fix while you're still moving. There are no sensors or wearables, and the camera feed stays on your device.

Our roots

Built on clinical and sports-science experience

Our team has deep roots in physiotherapy, sports science and rehabilitation. We draw on more than a decade of hands-on clinical experience and movement analysis.

That experience shapes how every movement is modeled. We look beyond where the joints are to what a small deviation means for injury risk and performance. And we build for the expert: MoveMentor extends what a coach or clinician can do, and they stay in charge.
