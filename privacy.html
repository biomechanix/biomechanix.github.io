Privacy Policy

Effective July 6, 2026 · Applies to the MoveMentor apps by Biomechanix Inc., including MoveMentor Fitness.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and name, used to create and secure your account.
  • Workout records: the sessions you log — exercise, reps, form scores, duration — linked to your account so your history follows you across devices.

What we do NOT collect

  • Camera video: pose analysis runs entirely on your device. Camera frames are never transmitted, stored, or seen by us.
  • Location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or browsing data.

How your data is used

Solely to provide app functionality: signing you in, syncing your workout history, and showing your progress. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes. Data is processed and stored with our infrastructure provider (Supabase).

Deleting your account

Open a support request from the email on your account and we will delete your account and all associated data.
