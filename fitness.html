MoveMentor Fitness · Training

Your camera becomes your coach.

Stand your phone up, step back, and get live coaching on every rep. MoveMentor Fitness sees your movement, counts your reps and tells you out loud when your form slips, so you can keep your eyes on the workout instead of the screen.

Get it on Google Play See what it does
What you get

Coaching that watches every rep, not just the timer.

01

Real-time form feedback

33 body landmarks are tracked 30 times a second. Each rep is scored against how the exercise should move: the joint that drives it, the joints that should stay still, and the common compensations.

02

Voice cues you can act on

Short spoken cues and haptics guide each phase: get set, go down, hold, come up. You'll hear a correction while there's still time to fix the next rep.

03

Automatic rep counting

Reps are counted from your movement, with no tapping. A rep only counts when you reach the target range, and holds are timed at the top.

04

Guided workouts

Preset routines for warm-up, full body, lower body, core and posture, and mobility, plus yoga poses and sport-specific programs. Levels run from beginner to advanced.

05

Perspiration Mode

A workout that makes music with your body. Your movements trigger beats and rhythms, so a sweat session turns into a jam session.

06

Progress you can see

Workout history, session summaries and form-quality trends over time. You can record short clips of your reps to your phone and review your technique.

Inside the app

Designed to stay out of your way.

Get started in a minute

Set up once, then just move.

  1. Prop up your phoneAt chest height, about 2–2.5 m (6–8 ft) away, with your whole body in view.
  2. Face the lightStand facing a window or lamp, not with the light behind you.
  3. Clear some spaceAbout 2 × 2 m (6 × 6 ft) is enough for every workout.
  4. Start a workoutLine up with the on-screen guide. The app waits until it can see you, then starts coaching.
Privacy first

Camera analysis never leaves your phone.

Camera frames are processed on the device and never uploaded. Rep clips are saved on your phone and shared only if you choose to. Your workout history syncs to your account, or use guest mode to try the app without signing up.

Good to know

Fitness, not medicine.

MoveMentor Fitness is a fitness and wellness app. It doesn't provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you're recovering from an injury, a physiotherapist using MoveMentor Physio can prescribe exercises for you.
