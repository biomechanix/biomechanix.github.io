Applications · built on MoveMentor

Three apps, one movement engine.

Every Biomechanix application runs on the MoveMentor platform. It uses the same on-device pose analysis and the same model of how a movement should look. Each app applies it to a different job: training, rehabilitation or self-assessment.

Training

MoveMentor Fitness

A personal trainer that can see you. You get live form feedback, automatic rep counting and spoken cues on every rep, with no wearables.

Explore Fitness Google Play
  • ForAnyone training at home or in the gym
  • The cameraScores your form, counts reps and times holds
  • HighlightsGuided workouts, yoga, sport-specific programs, Perspiration Mode
  • You getWorkout history and form-quality trends
Rehabilitation

MoveMentor Physio

For physiotherapy clinics and their patients. Examine with the camera, prescribe exercises at the right dose, and follow each patient's progress at home.

Explore Physio Google Play
  • ForPhysiotherapists and their patients
  • The cameraMeasures range of motion, symmetry and hold time, then guides home exercise
  • HighlightsContraindication checks, custom exercise authoring, QR or online delivery, works offline
  • You getExam findings, prescriptions, and adherence reports that go back to the clinician
Self-assessment

Sameeksha

A guided musculoskeletal self-screening for your knee, lower back, shoulder or elbow. It ends with a clear report and one recommended next step.

Explore Sameeksha Google Play
  • ForPeople with joint pain, on their own or helped by a caregiver
  • The cameraRuns guided movement tests and, for knee and back, a walking check
  • HighlightsSafety screen that always takes priority, six languages, PDF report
  • You getA screening report (not a diagnosis) with one clear next step
